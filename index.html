Monster. A Garden Cafe sits on the first floor of Central Market. Grab a buttery croffle and a garden soda, then spend the afternoon with our resident cats.

Our well-loved cats roam the cafe. Come for a coffee and you'll probably end up staying for them.

Croffles, sweet & savoury

Buttery croissant waffles topped with pistachio, salted miso caramel or creamy mushroom. Or try them with fried chicken.

Garden-fresh sips
